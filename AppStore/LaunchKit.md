## Description

> **Gong does one thing.**
>
> In the middle of your screen hangs a hand-hammered bronze gong, suspended on
> silk beneath a gilded beam. Touch it, and a ceremonial mallet draws back.
> Release, and it strikes.
>
> The sound is not a recording. Your gong is forged the moment the app opens —
> fourteen inharmonic partials, a post-strike bloom, a shimmer that breathes —
> and it rings down through a great hall for eighteen seconds. Hold longer,
> strike harder. Feel the contact in your palm.
>
> There are no levels. There are no streaks. There are no notifications
> begging you to return. There is a gong, and there is you, and there is the
> number of times you have struck it, kept in Roman numerals.
>
> **Gong costs $100 because it can.**
>
> And for the very few: **Gong Élite**. A one-time $999 ascension that
> re-finishes your instrument in 24-karat gold, retunes it to the brighter
> Aurum voice, turns every strike into white-gold radiance, and engraves the
> Élite crest beneath your disc. Forever. Everyone who sees your phone will
> know. That is the point.
>
> One sound. Infinite status.

## Review notes (for App Review)

- The app is intentionally single-purpose: it is a premium digital instrument,
  in the tradition of high-priced novelty/status apps. All advertised
  functionality (synthesized gong strike, animation, haptics) is fully
  delivered.
- The $999.99 non-consumable IAP is cosmetic + audio retuning ("Élite"
  finish). It is restorable via "Restore Purchases" in the boutique sheet.
- No accounts, no data collection, no network use beyond StoreKit.
