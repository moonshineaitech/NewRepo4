Frequently asked

How do I strike the gong?
Touch the gong in the middle of the screen. A mallet draws back while you hold — release to strike. Hold longer to strike harder.
Can I strike it with Siri or the Action button?
Yes. Say "Strike the Gong," or map the Shortcut to your iPhone's Action button in Settings → Action Button → Shortcut.
What is Gong Élite?
A one-time upgrade that re-finishes your instrument in 24-karat gold, retunes it to the brighter Aurum voice, and engraves the Élite crest. It is yours forever, across your devices.
I bought Élite but don't see the gold.
Open the crown (top-right) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
I want a refund.
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Does Gong collect my data?
No. See our Privacy Policy.
